    Questions

    Why do I need a Claude key?

    That's his brain. Your key lives only in a settings file on your PC and talks straight to Anthropic. It means there's no middleman reading your requests, and no subscription to us for something you'd pay for anyway.

    How much will Claude cost me?

    It depends entirely on how much you talk to him. Simple things like "pause" or "volume 30" are handled on your PC and cost nothing. He shows his own usage in Settings, and you can set a hard monthly limit in your Anthropic account so a surprise bill can't happen.

    Is there a free trial of Supporter?

    There's nothing to trial. Supporter adds no features. It's a way to pay for development if you want to.

    Does he work on Mac or Linux?

    Not right now. He's built deep into Windows: the wallpaper layer, the mic, the file index, the game detection.

    Can I cancel Supporter?

    Any time, from the same place you signed up. Nothing in the app changes when you do.

    Things he will never do.

    Type your passwords.

    Not into sites, not into apps, not for convenience.

    Trade or move money.

    He'll give you an opinion on a stock. He won't buy it.

    Click inside your games.

    He watches the scoreboard, not the controls.

    Read your browser logins or secret files.

    .ssh, password vaults, .env files are off limits.

    Talk in a real actor's voice.

    His voice is his own.

    Keep listening after you mute him.

    Muted means the mic is ignored entirely.

    sheet 6 of 6  assembly instructions

    Five minutes to hello.

    No account with us. You need a Windows PC, a microphone, and a Claude key.

    Windows 10 or 1164-bit
    A microphoneheadset, desk mic or webcam
    A Claude API keyfrom console.anthropic.com
    NVIDIA GPUoptional, makes hearing faster

    1. Download and run Setup

      Grab the installer from the download page. It installs to your user folder, no admin needed, and updates itself from then on.

    2. Get your Claude key

      Make an account at console.anthropic.com, add a little credit, create a key, and set a monthly limit while you're there.

    3. Paste it in Settings, Keys

      He checks it straight away. Optional: a free Groq key makes him understand you faster and more accurately.

    4. Say "Jarvis, learn my voice"

      Talk normally for about 20 seconds. From then on, he only answers you.

    5. Pick his world

      Settings, Desktop. He takes over your wallpaper and your icons stay where they are. If you use Wallpaper Engine, close it first: only one of them can live behind your icons.

    stuck? say Jarvis, run diagnostics and he'll tell you which part isn't working.
